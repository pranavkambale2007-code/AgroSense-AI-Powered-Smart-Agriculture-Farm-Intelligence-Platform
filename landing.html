THE AGRICULTURAL REALITY

Why Modern Farming Requires Integrated Decision Intelligence

Traditional farming relies heavily on seasonal memory and supplier advice. In an era of unpredictable monsoons, nutrient-depleted soils, and volatile wholesale markets, isolated decisions carry high economic risks.

Soil Nutrient Imbalance

Over-application of urea and unverified NPK ratios leads to soil acidification and low nutrient absorption, costing farmers up to ₹4,000/acre in wasted inputs.

Erratic Weather Shocks

Sudden temperature spikes and unseasonal rains during flowering stages destroy pollen fertility, triggering up to 35% crop yield loss without early mitigation.

Delayed Pathogen Diagnosis

Fungal blights and bacterial spots spread silently 5–7 days before extensive foliar lesions appear. Delayed chemical spraying allows infections to cross the economic injury threshold.

Mandi Price Opacity

Lack of visibility into daily APMC mandi modal prices and seasonal arrival trends forces growers into distress sales below Minimum Support Price (MSP) benchmarks.

Module 01 • Soil & Climate Synthesis

Empirical Crop Selection & Suitability Engine

69 Crop Agronomic Profiles

What it does: Analyzes your farm's empirical soil chemical attributes and 14-day weather forecasts to rank crops by physiological suitability, yield expectation, and projected profit.

Inputs used: Available Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K) in kg/ha, soil pH balance, Organic Carbon %, mean temperature (°C), relative humidity (%), expected rainfall (mm), and acreage.

Outputs produced: Ranked list of candidate crops with a suitability index (0–100%), projected quintals per acre, net profit margin estimate (₹), and an explainable breakdown of limiting soil or climatic factors.

How it helps: Prevents planting crops that will suffer nutrient lockout or heat stress, protecting the grower's capital before seed purchase.

Agronomic Note & Limitation: Suitability models reflect state agricultural university trial datasets; anomalous macro-climatic disasters should be confirmed against the Weather Risk Matrix.

What it does: Examines uploaded foliar photographs to identify crop pathologies, determine disease severity, and provide actionable Integrated Pest Management (IPM) protocols.

Inputs used: Clear foliar leaf image (JPG/PNG), selected or auto-detected crop species, and observed lesion patterns.

Outputs produced: Identified pathogen name, diagnostic confidence score (%), healthy reference baseline comparison, and three-pillar remedies (biological, chemical knapsack dosage, and cultural field practices).

How it helps: Catches fungal and bacterial outbreaks early, preventing secondary spread across neighbouring rows and saving input costs on ineffective fungicides.

Diagnostic Disclaimer: Visual computer vision analysis is an early screening aid; critical chemical spray programs should be verified with local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) extension officers.

Module 03 • Spatial Digital Twin

Interactive 3D Farm Simulator & Canopy Twin

HTML5 Canvas PBR Engine

What it does: Renders a real-time, interactive 3D procedural twin of your agricultural plot with volumetric plant canopies, soil stratum depths, and solar trajectory lighting.

Inputs used: Crop selection (100+ species supported), days after sowing (DAS), diurnal sun azimuth, and field zone irrigation status.

Outputs produced: 360° orbital inspection with pan, zoom, day/night lighting, drip irrigation line status, and root-zone moisture stratification.

How it helps: Gives agricultural consultants and growers spatial intuition for plant spacing, shade geometry, and soil moisture retention without physical field visits.

Zero-Dependency Architecture: Rendered client-side via optimized procedural canvas mathematics. Operates at a smooth 60 FPS on mobile phones, tablets, and budget laptops without requiring a dedicated GPU.
