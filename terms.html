3. Description of AgroSense Services

AgroSense is an open-source, client-side agricultural decision intelligence web application. Features include AI crop selection matrices, computer vision foliar disease screening, irrigation scheduling (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith formulas), 3D procedural plant growth simulations, and farm profitability calculators.

4. AI Crop Recommendations and Prediction Limitations

The Crop Recommendation Engine evaluates soil Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), soil pH, and regional rainfall against empirical agronomic matrices based on Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) guidelines.

Important Limitation:

Recommendations are statistical approximations. Crop performance depends on unmodeled local variables including seed quality, subsoil compaction, micronutrient status, pest pressures, and unseasonal weather shocks. AgroSense does not guarantee crop germination, harvest yield, or commercial return.

5. Plant Disease Detection Limitations

The Plant Disease Screening tool (#/disease) provides exploratory computer vision screening on leaf imagery.

  • Not a Laboratory Confirmation: In-browser visual analysis cannot replace laboratory molecular assays (PCR, ELISA) or physical soil pathogen tests.
  • Input Sensitivity: Image blur, poor illumination, leaf reflections, or atypical symptom expressions can produce false positive or false negative results.
  • Chemical Safety: Never apply concentrated chemical pesticides, fungicides, or restricted biological treatments solely on the basis of an algorithmic screening score without expert on-ground confirmation.
