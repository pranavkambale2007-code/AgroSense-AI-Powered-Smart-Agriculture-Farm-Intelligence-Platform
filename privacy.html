Privacy-By-Design Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 • Last Updated: October 23, 2026 • Version: 2.5.4

1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to AgroSense — Autonomous Agricultural Decision Intelligence Platform (“AgroSense”, “we”, “our”, or “the platform”). AgroSense is an open-source agricultural technology project and decision-support web application accessible at https://pranavkambale2007-code.github.io/AgroSense-AI-Powered-Smart-Agriculture-Farm-Intelligence-Platform/.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you interact with our website, use our client-side machine learning diagnostic tools, or utilize our agricultural decision support modules. We operate under a strict client-side processing architecture designed to minimize the collection and transmission of personal data.

2. Information We Collect

AgroSense does not require user account registration, passwords, payment details, national identity numbers, or social media logins to use any core functionality. Information handled falls into three distinct categories:

  • Agronomic parameters you explicitly enter into calculators (e.g. soil nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, field acreage, crop choice).
  • Ephemeral image streams captured via your camera or uploaded for disease diagnosis, which remain local to your browser.
  • Technical network requests generated when your browser downloads static web assets or requests public weather data from open APIs.

3. Information Users Provide

When utilizing AgroSense modules, you may enter details relating to your agricultural operations:

  • Soil chemistry values: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), Soil pH.
  • Geographical coordinates or district names for local weather retrieval.
  • Farm zoning labels (e.g., “Plot A - Kharif Maize”).
  • Economic parameters: seed cost, labor cost, irrigation expenses, expected yield.

Storage location: These inputs are stored exclusively within your web browser’s local storage (localStorage). They are not transmitted to or stored on any central AgroSense database.

4. Uploaded Crop Photographs and Camera Access

The Plant Disease Screening module (#/disease) provides real-time foliar pathology analysis using an HTML5 camera stream (navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia) or photo upload (<input type="file">).

Verified Technical Guarantee:

Uploaded leaf photographs and camera video frames never leave your device. The computer vision model runs in-browser using WebGL and Canvas operations. Your photographs are never uploaded, saved to a server, or shared with third parties.

Users are advised to focus images solely on plant leaves and crops, and to avoid capturing individuals, faces, identity documents, or sensitive personal property in the camera frame.

5. Farm, Crop, Soil, Weather, and Agricultural Data

Agricultural calculations (such as Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration, ICAR nutrient balance indexes, and growing-degree day trackers) are executed as mathematical formulas directly inside your browser’s JavaScript runtime.

To fetch real-time temperature, humidity, and precipitation data, the application queries the public Open-Meteo API using the latitude and longitude of your selected region. This request carries only spatial coordinates and contains no identifying personal information.

6. Technical Information, Browser Data, and Diagnostics

Like any website hosted on standard web infrastructure (GitHub Pages / GitHub Inc.), the hosting server automatically receives standard web request metadata, including:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and user agent string.
  • Date and time of static asset requests.
  • Referrer URLs.

AgroSense maintainers do not operate access-log collection servers or aggregate user diagnostic fingerprints.

7. Cookies, Local Storage, Session Storage, and Similar Technologies

HTTP Cookies: AgroSense does not set or read any HTTP cookies. We do not use cookie-based session tracking, advertising cookies, or cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

Browser Local Storage (localStorage): We utilize the following specific browser keys to retain your work locally between visits:

Storage Key Category Purpose
agrosense_farms Strictly Necessary Stores user-defined farm polygons, acreage, and crop tags.
agrosense_active_farm_id Strictly Necessary Maintains selection of the active farm zone across modules.
agrosense_notifications Functional Stores local agronomic advisory logs and threshold alerts.
agrosense_profit_params Functional Retains input parameters for the Profitability Calculator.
agrosense_pending_sync_queue Strictly Necessary Maintains local offline task queues when disconnected.
agrosense_cookie_consent Strictly Necessary Remembers user privacy and local storage consent preferences.

You can clear these values at any time through your browser's “Clear Site Data” or “Clear Storage” settings.

8. Why Information Is Processed

Information processed on your device is strictly utilized to:

  • Provide real-time soil nutrient deficiency and crop suitability matrices.
  • Render 3D interactive visualizations of plant developmental biology.
  • Calculate water conservation and irrigation schedules.
  • Retain your agricultural inputs across navigation sessions on the same browser.

9. AI Inference and Model Processing

AgroSense uses client-side machine learning inference for foliar symptom detection. Model weights and configuration layers are served as static pre-compiled JSON/binary assets and evaluated via client-side WebGL.

Probabilistic Notice: All AI outputs (including confidence scores and pathogen matches) are statistical estimations. They are designed as informational aids and should not be treated as infallible botanical certifications or laboratory assays.

10. Third-Party Services and External APIs

To deliver full functionality without a custom centralized backend, AgroSense connects directly from your browser to specific public third-party services:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Static website hosting, CDN delivery, and edge routing.
  • Open-Meteo API: Retrieves free, non-commercial micro-meteorological forecasts based on coordinates.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Geocodes village and district queries into latitude/longitude coordinates.
  • Leaflet CDN (unpkg.com) & Cloudflare CDN: Delivers GIS mapping and Three.js 3D rendering scripts.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com): Serves typographic web fonts.

Each external provider operates under its respective privacy terms. AgroSense does not supply personal identifying data to any of these endpoints.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Because AgroSense operates without a user account database, we do not hold your data on our servers. All saved farms and calculator configurations reside strictly in your web browser’s storage.

How to erase your data: You can completely erase all AgroSense data at any time by selecting “Clear Browsing Data / Storage” in your web browser or clicking the “Reset All Farm Data” option in the AgroSense farm settings view.

12. Data Sharing and Disclosures

AgroSense does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade user data. We do not provide user information to commercial data brokers, advertising agencies, or marketing networks.

13. Security Practices and Limitations

Our primary security measure is architectural: by keeping sensitive calculations, images, and farm profiles client-side, we prevent central data breaches. All website traffic is served over encrypted Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS).

Limitation: You are responsible for maintaining the physical and logical security of your own device, browser, and local storage.

14. Children's Privacy

AgroSense is intended for adult farmers, agronomists, researchers, and students. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

15. User Rights and Privacy Requests

In accordance with international privacy principles (including the EU GDPR and the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):

  • Right of Access & Portability: All your farm profiles and records can be viewed and copied directly from the browser interface.
  • Right of Erasure: You can purge all stored records instantly by clearing your browser cache and local storage.
  • Right of Rectification: You can edit or modify farm coordinates, soil values, and crop profiles directly within the UI.

16. International Transfers

As a statically hosted website distributed over international Content Delivery Networks (GitHub Pages), web assets are delivered from global edge servers. Weather queries to Open-Meteo are transmitted to their European server infrastructure without personal identifiers.

17. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, model upgrades, or architectural adjustments. The updated revision date at the top of this document indicates the current effective version.
