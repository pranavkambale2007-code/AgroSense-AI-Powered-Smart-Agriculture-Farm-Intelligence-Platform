Cookie Policy & Local Storage Notice

Last Updated: October 23, 2026 • Status: Zero HTTP Cookies Active

1. Cookies vs. Local Storage: What Is the Difference?

Many websites use HTTP cookies—small text strings sent back and forth between your browser and their web servers on every single network request. Cookies are commonly used for cross-site behavioral tracking and commercial advertising.

AgroSense does NOT use HTTP cookies. Our server does not set Set-Cookie headers, and our scripts do not write to document.cookie.

Instead, we rely on standard HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage). Local storage is kept isolated inside your browser and is never automatically transmitted to any server during web requests.

2. Audit of Local Storage Keys Actually Used

The table below provides a complete, verified audit of all storage keys utilized by the AgroSense application:

Storage Key Category Purpose Lifespan
agrosense_farms Strictly Necessary Saves user-drawn GIS farm polygons, soil parameters, and crop rotation schedules on device. Persistent until cleared
agrosense_active_farm_id Strictly Necessary Preserves the currently selected active farm ID across navigation views. Persistent until cleared
agrosense_pending_sync_queue Strictly Necessary Stores offline action queues when working in rural areas with intermittent connectivity. Persistent until cleared
agrosense_cookie_consent Strictly Necessary Remembers your privacy preferences so the notice banner is not displayed repeatedly. Persistent until cleared
agrosense_profit_params Functional Preference Remembers custom input parameters in the Profitability & Cost Estimation Calculator. Persistent until cleared
agrosense_notifications Functional Preference Caches agronomic advisory logs and threshold notices for your farm zones. Persistent until cleared
agrosense_satellite_credentials Functional Preference Locally holds optional user-supplied Copernicus or Sentinel satellite access tokens. Persistent until cleared

3. What Technologies We Do NOT Use

To protect your privacy and ensure transparent compliance with the EU ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, and Indian DPDP Act, AgroSense explicitly excludes:

  • No Advertising Cookies: No Google AdSense, DoubleClick, or social network remarketing pixels.
  • No Behavioral Analytics Trackers: No Google Analytics (GA4), Mixpanel, Hotjar, or session-replay recorders.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: No canvas fingerprinting or hardware identification scripts.
  • No Commercial Consent Management Vendor Trackers: Our consent controller is an open-source, client-side script (dist/consent.js) that introduces zero third-party calls.

4. Managing and Clearing Your Local Storage

You have total control over all information stored on your device:

  • Via the AgroSense Consent Modal: Click the button at any time to toggle optional functional presets on or off.
  • Via Your Web Browser: You can completely purge all stored farm models and parameters by opening your browser's Developer Tools or Settings menu → “Privacy & Security” → “Clear Browsing Data” → selecting “Cookies and other site data”.

5. Policy Updates and Contact

If our storage keys or technical architecture change in future software releases, this document will be updated accordingly. For inquiries or technical questions, refer to our Contact Page or submit an issue via our open-source GitHub Issue Tracker.
