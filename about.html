Mission & Scientific Architecture

Autonomous Decision Intelligence Grounded in Real Agronomic Science.

AgroSense was created to address one of modern farming's greatest challenges: fragmented agricultural data. By bridging soil chemistry, micro-meteorology, foliar pathology, and farm telemetry into an integrated, client-side platform, AgroSense empowers farmers, students, and agronomists with explainable, empirical recommendations.

Four Foundational Pillars

Engineered with verified agronomic algorithms and zero-cloud client privacy.

1. AI Crop Recommendation Engine

Evaluates primary soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), soil pH, and seasonal precipitation using a 46-crop multivariate agronomic matrix. Calibrated to Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) trial thresholds to prevent fertilizer waste and maximize natural yield potential.

2. Client-Side Foliar Disease Detection

Provides instant, private computer vision screening for foliar plant pathogens. Runs entirely in your browser using client-side WebGL acceleration. Leaf images and camera video frames remain 100% on your device, ensuring complete farmer privacy without requiring cloud image uploads.

3. Precision Evapotranspiration & Irrigation

Computes daily reference crop evapotranspiration (ET0) using the internationally recognized FAO-56 Penman-Monteith methodology. Balances root-zone soil water depletion against local micro-meteorological forecasts (Open-Meteo) to eliminate over-irrigation and conserve groundwater.

4. 3D Digital Twin & Procedural Simulation

Brings agronomy to life through realistic 3D simulations. From the interactive village farm landscape of rural Maharashtra to the seed-to-harvest procedural growth story (tracking root draw-range, leaf unfurling, and cob fill), AgroSense visualizes agricultural biology with cinematic clarity.

Zero-Cloud Architecture & Privacy-First Design

Unlike conventional agricultural platforms that harvest farmer data into centralized corporate databases, AgroSense is engineered under a Zero-CORS, static edge architecture:

No Central Database

All farm zones, acreages, and economic params reside strictly in your browser’s localStorage.

Zero Tracking Cookies

No Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or third-party behavioral fingerprinting. No marketing cookies are ever set.

Offline-First Capable

Once cached, core calculators and 3D simulation modules operate seamlessly in remote fields without network connectivity.

Scientific References & Agronomic Standards

  • ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research): Soil nutrient indices, regional crop agro-climatic zones, and recommended fertilizer application rates.
  • FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56: Crop evapotranspiration guidelines for computing crop water requirements under standard conditions.
  • AgMIP (Agricultural Model Intercomparison and Improvement Project): Climate-crop interaction modeling protocols.
  • e-NAM (National Agriculture Market): Daily wholesale mandi APMC price discovery reference tables.
